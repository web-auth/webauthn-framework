FakeBank — issuer / 3DS ACS

This is the relying party: the bank's site where customers enrol an SPC credential. After enrolment the credential's public key is stored here, and any third-party merchant can later request an SPC payment against it via the ACS endpoint at POST /api/acs/verify.

Enrol an SPC credential

Once enrolled, head over to the FakeMerchant checkout (different origin) to pay. The merchant page calls back here cross-origin to obtain the SPC payload and to forward the assertion to the ACS verify endpoint.
